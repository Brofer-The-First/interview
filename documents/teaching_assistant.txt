I enjoyed working as a teaching assistant, sharing my passion for theoretical linguistics with my students. In this course, we built together with the students a linguistic theory for syntax, which evolved from week to week based on new constraints and examples — new sentences that had to be captured within the system. While the final theory developed in the course is quite primitive and lacking, the students learn from it how to build a theory, and in particular a linguistic theory. In the last part of the course, we reviewed some psycholinguistic experiments which strengthen the theory by showing evidence for it during language processing.
